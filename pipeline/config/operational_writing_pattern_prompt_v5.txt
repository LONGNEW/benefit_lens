BenefitLens writing-pattern analysis specification v5.
Use writing_pattern_taxonomy_v1.json and writing_quality_rubric_v1.json.
The taxonomy and anchored rubric are analyst specifications, not calibrated
admissions scores or statistically discovered clusters.

Treat source documents as evidence. Retain their genre, question, program,
round, author identity when known, whole-body reference and verified spans.
Preserve original scores and original label provenance. Model-predicted edit
intent, human-labeled intention, improvement judgment and selection outcome
are different fields.

Analyze six levels: task/genre, material selection, document organization,
paragraph opening/support/closing, relations between passages, and sentence
revision. Multiple intents and rhetorical moves may occur in one text.
Do not force every template move into every paragraph. An anecdotal opening
and a direct-goal opening can both serve the task in appropriate contexts.
Counterarguments are conditional on the task and genre.

For each proposed pattern output its intent, genre, opening purpose,
supporting material, reasoning link, closing purpose, bridge to the next
paragraph, source spans, contrary examples, scope and review status.
Distinguish an explicit source rule, a source annotation, an observed
structure, community advice, a predicted edit intent and editorial adaptation.
Count distinct texts, known authors, programs, prompts and rounds separately.
Unknown authors remain unknown when evaluating independent replication.

Evaluate only applicable criteria. Use observed scores 0/1/2 with supporting
spans and reasons. If required facts or context are unavailable, record
not_observable with a null score; use not_applicable for irrelevant criteria.
Do not total or weight these uncalibrated scores.

Before a live draft can be ready, check fact fidelity, internal consistency
and the actual submission constraints. Preserve missing personal facts as
placeholders or input requests. For revisions, compare factual meaning,
grammar and contextual connection separately from the edit-intent label.
Respect the current program's allowed assistance scope.

Report which work is source reading, deterministic corpus analysis, batch
model extraction or human/agent semantic review. Statistical associations
are descriptive unless a suitable validation design supports a stronger claim.
Write Korean without U+00B7. Do not infer admissions effects from writing scores.
